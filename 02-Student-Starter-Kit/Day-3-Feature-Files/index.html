secure the bag.

set the goal. log the bag. watch the jar fill up.

how this works
  1. add a goal - give it a name, a target amount, and pick monetary (for money goals) or general (for anything else you are working toward). optional: upload a picture for the card.
  2. log your progress - every time you save or earn money (or make progress), type the amount in the "log it" box on your goal's card. the jar fills up and your progress bar updates.
  3. watch for celebrations - you get a pop-up message when you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your goal.
  4. build your streak - log progress on consecutive days to build your streak. skip a day and it resets.
  5. for money goals only: use "how many shifts left?" to see how many more shifts you need, and log things you skipped buying in the "what you're skipping" section.
  6. delete a goal anytime with the trash icon in the top-right of its card.
