## How your file talks to the rest of the app
Every feature file uses the same two building blocks:

1. **Events** - `core.js` announces things happening ("a goal was
   just created", "a card was just drawn", "progress was just
   logged"). Your file listens for the announcement it cares
   about and reacts.
2. **Shared functions and variables** - `goals`, `save()`,
   `render()`, and `showToast()` are all available to use
   directly in your file, since every script on the page shares
   the same JavaScript world.

Open your assigned file and we will walk through it together.

## Testing your feature
Open `index.html` in your browser after saving your file. Add a
goal and try your feature. If nothing happens, open your
browser's developer console (usually F12) to see if there's an
error message telling you what went wrong.
